Golden Goose

Synthetic stock: a bullish trade that acts like owning the stock for 1–2 years, for a small part of the cost.

? What it is

  • Stock = Call − Put. Buying a call and selling a put behaves like owning the stock.
  • The short put spread brings in money to pay for the trade.
  • The long call is the part that makes the money.
  • 3-leg GG: put spread + long call. You can sell monthly covered calls against it.
  • 4-leg GG: put spread + long call spread. Much cheaper, but the upside is capped and you can't sell covered calls.
  • Goal: pay little or no time value (extrinsic) for the whole trade.

🔎 Pick the stock

  • Only stocks you expect to go up over 1–2 years. GG does not work as a bearish trade.
  • Good places to look: IBD 50, your favorite stocks, strong stocks beaten down after earnings, stocks in a squeeze.
  • Options must be liquid (small bid/ask gap).
  • Average volume over 1 million shares a day.
  • Stock price over $30.

🟢 Enter the trade

  1. Expiration: 1 year or more out (usually about 2 years).
  2. Sell a put spread. Short put = first strike out of the money. Long put about $20 lower (stocks $70–$150; use about $50 for very pricey stocks). Aim for a credit of 30–50% of the width ($7–$10 on a $20 spread).
  3. Buy a call 1–2 strikes in the money. Subtract the put spread's time value from the call's time value. If only a few dollars are left, stop here: 3-leg GG.
  4. 4-leg only: sell a call higher up whose time value is about the same as what was left in step 3.
  5. Check it: do the max profit, max loss and upside make sense?
  6. Size it: the max loss is real. Trade only as many contracts as you can afford to lose.
Example order (AMD, 4-leg)BUY +1 1/-1/-1/1 CUSTOM AMD 100 20 JAN 23/20 JAN 23/20 JAN 23/20 JAN 23 82.5/135/85/65 CALL/CALL/PUT/PUT @2.33 LMT GTC

💰 Take profits

Set these GTC closing orders the day the trade fills. They often fill at the open while you're busy.

  • Stock goes up → close the put spread at 50% of max profit. This removes the risk and frees the margin for new trades.
  • Stock goes down → 4-leg: buy back the short call at 50% profit. Then you can start selling monthly covered calls. (At 30–40% you can close it and sell a call 1 month out.)
  • Stock goes way up → close the long call spread as one unit for about $5 less than its width. Example: 80/45 spread = $35 max, so a GTC order at $30.
  • Link the short-call close and the call-spread close with OCO (one cancels the other).
  • Never sell the long call by itself — that leaves a dangerous naked call.

📅 Monthly income (3-leg)

  • Sell a call about 1 month out (monthly options), at delta 20% or less.
  • Ask for the premium of a delta 30% call, and only if it pays $1.00 or more.
  • Queue a GTC order to buy it back at 80% profit.
  • Stock near the strike? Roll to next month, higher strike, for a credit. If that's hard, roll out to the GG expiration.
  • Assigned (short stock in your account)? Buy the short stock back right away.

🛑 Close the whole trade

  • Big profit you're afraid to lose — take it and start a new trade.
  • Losses you can't take any longer (stock gapped down and keeps falling) — sell the long call and buy back the put spread.
  • About 6 months before expiration — time decay speeds up, so take the profit off the table.
  • Want long-term capital gains first? Keep the call, and sell an out-of-the-money call to slow the decay.
  • Don't roll to next year — it costs money. Open a new Golden Goose instead.

⭐ Golden rules

  • Never hold a naked call or naked put.
  • Know your max loss before you enter.
  • Queue every exit with GTC orders — no analysis paralysis.
  • Be patient: these trades last 1–2 years.
  • Track your trades (spreadsheet, notes).
  • Paper trade a new strategy before using real money.

📖 How to use this app

The 6 buttons at the bottom open the 6 pages. Here's what each one does.

💰 Trades
  • ＋ Add → paste one or more thinkorswim orders (one per line), pick the date, add a note, tap Add.
  • Copy puts a trade back on your clipboard, ready to paste into thinkorswim. Copy all copies the whole list.
  • Type a symbol in the search box to find a trade.
  • Tap OPEN to close a trade; it turns red CLOSED. Tap a trade to edit, reopen or delete it.
🗺️ Where We Live
  • Everyone's gold-coin pin, each trader in their own color. Pinch to zoom.
  • Tap your own pin to fix your location.
  • 👥 in the group opens the Traders page. ✈️ traveling lists who's away; tap a name to fly there.
  • Travelers show their local time vs ET / CT / PT — 😴 means they're probably asleep.
  • ⬅️ Back to main map zooms back out to everyone.
  • Right side: + / − zoom, 🌍 show everyone, layers satellite view, ✈️ add or hide trips.
💃 Traders
  • A sticky note for every friend. Search by name or city.
  • Tap a note to see that person; tap the ✈️ city tag to see where they're traveling.
  • ＋ Join (or My pin) takes you to Settings to add or fix your pin.
🥚 Golden Goose
  • The Golden Goose rules: what it is, picking a stock, entering, taking profits, monthly income and closing. Tap a section to open it.
📅 Next Meeting
  • The date and time of the next group meeting, in ET / CT / MT / PT, with a countdown.
  • 🎥 Join on Zoom opens the meeting; Copy Zoom link copies it; Add to my calendar saves it to your phone's calendar.
  • Set / Change the meeting to update it for everyone.
⚙️ Settings
  • 📱 Add app to your phone — put the app on your Home Screen.
  • My pin — join the map: type your name, tap I'm home (or type your ZIP). Move or change it later here.
  • 🗑️ Delete a pin — remove a pin put in the wrong place.
  • ✈️ Trips — tap Back home when someone returns. 🖨️ Print trades — print or save as PDF.
🔒 Locking
  • So nothing changes by accident, trades lock after 24 hours, pins and trips after 48 hours.
  • To change something older, tap Unlock (or use Editing lock here in Settings).
  • While editing is on, a gold Editing ON badge blinks at the top right — tap it to lock when you're done.

💡 With no internet you can still look at everything saved on your phone; adding or changing things needs internet.
